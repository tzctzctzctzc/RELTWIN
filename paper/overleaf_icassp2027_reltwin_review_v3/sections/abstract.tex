\begin{abstract}
Audio temporal grounding must distinguish windows containing the same sound categories in different orders. We introduce RelTwin, which places inverse relations in one recording and jointly supervises candidate discrimination and exchange consistency. Setwise Preference Optimization (SetPO) extends supervision to interval-set quality. With matched data, update counts, and seed, the joint objective improves over sequence supervision by 0.87 mIoU points on SpotSound-Bench and 13.44 points on class-disjoint relation development data, reducing window-confusion errors by 30.63 points. SetPO continuation adds 3.36 development mIoU points across three seeds. Paired audio- and source-cluster analyses support these controlled stage gains. The full pipeline reaches 59.39\% SpotSound mIoU, 1.49 points above the cited prior main-table best. We distinguish joint-training evidence from exchange-specific attribution and natural-relation transfer, which remain unverified.
\end{abstract}
