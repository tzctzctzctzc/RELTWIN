\section{Results and Discussion}
\subsection{Does joint training outperform sequence supervision?}
Table~\ref{tab:stages} places the largest observed public training-stage gain at RBEE: 59.166\% mean mIoU versus 58.317\% for the same-harness official model. All three RBEE seeds exceed this baseline. At seed~0, RBEE scores 59.183894\%, versus 58.313494\% for matched SFT, a 0.870400-point gain. Sequence supervision on the same paired examples does not reproduce the joint objective's observed score.

The newly aligned SFT row in Table~\ref{tab:relations} connects this comparison to its intended ability. At the same seed, RBEE improves relation mIoU by 13.44 points, reduces Swap error by 30.63 points, and raises JointPairAcc by 29.38 points. Only one of 160 RBEE seed-0 pairs passes PairAcc but fails JointPairAcc; no such discrepancy occurs in the other six adapted checkpoints. The result is therefore not explained by the loose threshold alone. These observations support the candidate/exchange combination, not exchange regularization independently; the SFT comparison has only one seed.

The historical hierarchical seed--record bootstrap interval for public RBEE minus official is $[-0.221,+1.965]$ points; SetPO minus RBEE is $[-0.313,+0.412]$ with mean +0.027. Public gains remain point estimates, not established significant effects. SetPO's main observed benefit is instead on relation development data: +3.36 mIoU, +5.21 PairAcc, $-2.71$ Swap, and +5.42 JointPairAcc points.

\subsection{Does the relation gain survive dependency-aware analysis?}
We replace the historical row-wise relation bootstrap with 20,000 paired cluster draws. All inverse queries and templates from one recording move together; the same draws are shared across models and the observed fixed seeds. Original ESC-50 recordings are reused across synthetic examples: grouping connected recordings by original Freesound ID, including different takes, yields 39 source components from 80 audio files (largest: 18 files). Source-component resampling provides a dependence sensitivity check.

Audio-cluster 95\% mIoU intervals are $[8.84,18.20]$ for seed-0 RBEE minus SFT and $[2.04,4.88]$ for three-seed SetPO minus RBEE. Source clustering gives $[8.65,17.14]$ and $[2.12,5.08]$, respectively. JointPairAcc intervals are also positive in both analyses. The intervals condition on observed seeds, not the seed population, and do not remove development selection. They support controlled relation-stage gains, not natural-relation generalization.

\subsection{Attribution gaps and targeted controls}
RBEE adds candidate discrimination and exchange together; SetPO changes the objective and adds 64 updates. Frozen three-seed controls remove only exchange while retaining candidate cross-entropy, or continue the RBEE objective for 64 steps at the same subsequent learning rate. Full relation/SpotSound evaluation will use one evaluator. These update-matched, not FLOP-matched, controls remain pending and are excluded from reported results. They address exchange-specific attribution and the extra-update explanation, respectively.

\subsection{System performance and scope}
Table~\ref{tab:main} compares the full pipeline with prior published main-table results, not our earlier checkpoints. SpotSound mIoU, R1@.3, and R1@.5 exceed the cited best by 1.49, 2.75, and 3.00 points. The final 59.385511\% includes routing and boundary refinement; refinement adds only 0.056963 points over E004. Clotho reaches 86.855592\%, but its official incumbent already scores 86.854263\%; its recalls remain below the cited best. Neither Clotho nor protocol-qualified UnAV uses a RelTwin incumbent, so neither establishes relation-training transfer.

RelTwin uses fixed templates, repeated excerpts, and silence-separated two-event windows; overlap, nested relations, and natural-recording transfer remain untested. Better relation scores do not prove a causal explanation of all public gains. Other system evaluations retain limitations: AudioGrounding/AEGBench informed development, with AEGBench release differences~\cite{zhang2026autoaeg}; DESED/TUT trail published references, and LAT exposes long-audio localization failure. Boundary editing cannot recover missing events; its radius remains 0.25 seconds throughout SpotSound and Clotho.
