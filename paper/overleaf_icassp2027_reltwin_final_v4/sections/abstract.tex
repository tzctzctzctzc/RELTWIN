\begin{abstract}
Audio temporal grounding must distinguish a query's answer from other real event windows, not merely detect whether its sounds occur. We introduce RelTwin, a paired construction in which inverse relations coexist in one recording but identify different timestamp answers. Contrasting these answers augments ordinary sequence supervision without adding inference modules. On a class- and source-disjoint synthetic development split, a matched-runtime, equal-update comparison improves mIoU from 74.58 to 88.55 over SFT. Across three seeds, candidate-supervised models reach 59.16 mIoU on SpotSound-Bench, compared with the cited prior main-table best of 57.9. Controlled comparisons do not establish separate benefits from exchange regularization or setwise continuation beyond simpler alternatives. Our findings support query-specific answer supervision and motivate diagnostics that require correct bidirectional localization, while distinguishing controlled development gains from public system performance and natural-scene generalization.
\end{abstract}
