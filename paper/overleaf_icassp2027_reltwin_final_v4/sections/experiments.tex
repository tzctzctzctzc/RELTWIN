\section{Experimental Setup}
\textbf{Data and split roles.}
Adaptation uses 40 ESC-50 classes and 256 constructed recordings, yielding 1,024 relation queries (512 paired examples) and 512 category-rehearsal queries. The other 10 classes provide 80 recordings, 320 queries, and 160 inverse pairs. Adaptation and development share neither classes nor original Freesound recording IDs. This split does not imply absence from backbone pretraining. The relation split has already informed method development; all its results are development evidence, not a fresh natural-scene test. Public evaluation uses all 400 SpotSound queries over 387 audio files. Public results have also been inspected during project development; no pristine held-out selection claim is made.

\textbf{Matched adaptation.}
All variants start from the released SpotSound-A adapter on Audio Flamingo~3 and update its 20,185,088 adapter parameters. SFT, Cand, and RBEE use 256 updates, AdamW with zero weight decay, learning rate $5\times10^{-6}$, gradient clipping at 1.0, $\tau=1$, $\lambda=1$, and $\beta=0.5$. Cand and RBEE have seeds 0/1/2. We retrain SFT seed~0 in the current environment with the same source code, initial weights, data order, and configuration as Cand seed~0; only the training objective differs. This is the primary attribution comparison. It controls update count, not FLOPs: candidate learning scores four answers rather than the two positive answers used by SFT.

RBEE continuation and SetPO each add 64 updates at $2\times10^{-6}$ from the corresponding RBEE parent. SetPO uses method weight 0.5, target temperature 0.15, exchange weight 1, and rehearsal weight 0.5; every fourth update includes setwise rehearsal. Final checkpoints are used without seed selection. The historical SFT, RBEE, and SetPO checkpoints are re-evaluated with the current harness; sharing inference does not establish identical historical training runtimes. Their contrasts are reported separately from the newly bridged SFT--Cand comparison.

\textbf{Uncertainty and reproducibility.}
We validate row indices, queries, targets, audio identity, duration, and recomputed IoU before comparison. Paired bootstrap uses 20,000 shared audio-group draws; inverse queries and templates stay together. Because some development recordings reuse excerpts, we also bootstrap 39 connected groups of original source recordings (largest: 18 constructed audios). Intervals condition on observed seeds, not the population of possible training seeds. Source grouping addresses reuse dependence but not development selection. Training/inference code, inputs, weights, and environment are hashed; one fresh SFT bridge and its 10-query smoke checks precede full 320/400 evaluation.
