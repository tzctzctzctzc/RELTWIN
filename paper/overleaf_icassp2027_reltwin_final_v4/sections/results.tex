\subsection{Are exchange and setwise extensions necessary?}
Table~\ref{tab:controls} prevents attributing every stage difference to a novel loss. Across three seeds, Cand and RBEE reach 87.05 and 86.78 relation mIoU, and 81.46 and 81.67 JointPairAcc. The JS increment is $-0.27$ mIoU and $+0.21$ JointPairAcc points; both source-group intervals cross zero. This does not prove equivalence, but it gives no basis for making exchange regularization the core empirical claim. The simpler candidate-supervised design is sufficient to retain the observed development performance.

SetPO improves over its RBEE parents by 3.04 mIoU points. However, continuing RBEE alone recovers 2.27 of those points. Against that equal-update control, SetPO's advantage is only 0.77 mIoU and 1.04 JointPairAcc points, again with intervals crossing zero. On SpotSound its equal-update difference is 0.019 points. Consequently, we do not describe SetPO as the main source of public improvement or its parent comparison as an isolated objective-function gain. The historical-training caveat further limits both extension contrasts; it does not convert their uncertain increments into positive evidence.

\subsection{Public scores answer a different question}
Table~\ref{tab:main} situates the work against the prior published \emph{main-table} results. Cand and RBEE average 59.16 and 59.29 SpotSound mIoU, above the cited 57.9. Our own official re-evaluation is already 58.42, so the entire literature gap cannot be attributed to adaptation. The matched within-harness comparison, rather than this cross-paper difference, supplies the training-effect evidence. Cand improves over official by 0.75 points across three observed seeds, with paired audio-group CI $[-0.24,1.74]$; this is not a statistically established public gain.

The archived full SpotSound system scores 59.39/79.25/62.50 in mIoU/R1@.3/R1@.5, exceeding the cited previous best by 1.49/2.75/3.00 points. It additionally routes official, SFT, and SetPO answers using keep/drop verification fitted on LongNeedle-ESC50, followed by a conservative local Boundary Utility stage. The latter changes 59.328548 to 59.385511 mIoU and cannot explain the full literature gap. These archived predictions are not mixed with current single-checkpoint predictions to estimate component effects.

Clotho's 86.86 mIoU uses the official incumbent plus the same type of boundary refinement, without RelTwin adaptation; its unrefined incumbent is already 86.854263. Its two recall measures are slightly below the cited best. We retain it as clearly labeled system context, not evidence of cross-benchmark relation transfer. UnAV is omitted from fair ranking because its public 100-query manifest has not been reconciled with the source paper's reported protocol.

\subsection{Evidence boundaries}
The controlled relation split measures binding between simple sequential descriptions and constructed windows. It does not establish robustness to natural, overlapping, nested, or long-range relations. Repeated excerpts, fixed wording, silence, and layout-dependent gaps can supply shortcuts. Class/source separation and source-group bootstrap address two specific concerns, but do not remove these construction biases or prior development selection.

Our re-evaluation also changes some historical predictions: SFT seed~0 differs on 49 of 400 SpotSound queries despite matching input token counts. We therefore replace stale stage comparisons with one aligned current cohort and add the matched-runtime bridge, rather than treating execution drift as an algorithmic gain. The primary SFT--Cand comparison remains one seed. Larger independent natural-relation validation, not additional system modules, is the principal unresolved generalization requirement.
