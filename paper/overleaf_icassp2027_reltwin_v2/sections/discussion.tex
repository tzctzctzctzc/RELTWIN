\section{Results and Discussion}
\subsection{Where does the public-set gain occur?}
Table~\ref{tab:stages} locates the largest observed training-stage gain at RBEE: 59.166\% mean mIoU versus 58.317\% for the same-harness official model, a 0.849-point increase. All three RBEE seeds exceed this baseline. At seed~0, RBEE scores 59.183894\%, versus 58.313494\% for the matched-configuration SFT control. Providing the paired examples with sequence supervision alone therefore does not reproduce the observed RBEE score. This supports the combined candidate/exchange objective, but does not isolate exchange regularization from candidate discrimination.

The existing hierarchical seed--record bootstrap interval for RBEE minus official is $[-0.221,+1.965]$ points. Hence the public gain is a directionally consistent three-seed point estimate, not an established significant improvement. SetPO adds only 0.027 points over RBEE, with interval $[-0.313,+0.412]$. Its public-set increment is not the primary evidence for structured preference training.

\subsection{Do inverse queries select the right windows?}
Table~\ref{tab:relations} tests SetPO on class-disjoint RelTwin development data against each matched RBEE parent. Query mIoU increases from 86.55\% to 89.90\%, while PairAcc@.5 rises from 81.46\% to 86.67\%. Swap error falls from 8.54\% to 5.83\%. Thus the improvement is not confined to average overlap: more inverse-query pairs correctly localize both relation-specific targets.

The historical bootstrap intervals are $[+2.469,+4.275]$ points for mIoU and $[+2.917,+7.708]$ for PairAcc. These resample query rows or relation pairs independently within each fixed seed and average across seeds; they do not fully preserve shared-audio/template dependence. We report the original uncertainty scope rather than treating these intervals as audio-cluster significance tests. The split also served development selection. The supported conclusion is improved controlled relation localization, not confirmed generalization to arbitrary natural relations.

\subsection{System performance versus training attribution}
Table~\ref{tab:main} compares the full pipeline against published main-table results, not our earlier checkpoints. On SpotSound, mIoU, R1@.3, and R1@.5 exceed the cited best values by 1.49, 2.75, and 3.00 points. The final 59.385511\% mIoU includes both routing and boundary refinement; the latter adds 0.056963 points over the locked E004 incumbent. These system numbers are distinct from the training-stage comparison.

Clotho reaches 86.855592\% mIoU, above the cited 85.6\% reference; the official incumbent already scores 86.854263\%. This small refinement increment cannot validate RBEE, which was not used as the incumbent. Clotho's R1@.3/.5 remain below the cited best values. UnAV also uses the official incumbent and carries the protocol qualification above. Neither column tests transfer of relation training.

The largest public training gain and the improved relation diagnostics are complementary observations. They do not establish that improved relational reasoning causes all SpotSound gains: the public benchmark is not a dedicated inverse-query test, and SetPO is the stage isolated in the relation comparison. Natural-recording relation evaluation and separation of candidate discrimination from exchange regularization remain necessary to connect that causal argument.

\subsection{Scope and remaining failures}
RelTwin uses two-event orders, fixed templates, repeated excerpts, and silence-separated windows. It does not cover arbitrary compositional queries, overlap, or nested relations. The class-disjoint split tests adaptation beyond its training categories, not an unseen foundation-model vocabulary. Exchange consistency is a regularizer, not a guarantee of correct outputs.

Additional system evaluations expose limits rather than establish RBEE transfer. AudioGrounding-v2 (70.47\%) and AEGBench public v3 (41.24\%) informed radius selection and are development evidence. The 9,924-query AEGBench release differs from the 9,790-query paper evaluation~\cite{zhang2026autoaeg}. DESED/TUT score 57.00/24.76\%, below the 61.1/33.2\% literature references, with assembly qualifications; LAT English TAG scores 15.14\%. Boundary editing cannot recover missing events. Its adaptive radius equals the fixed 0.25-second cap on both SpotSound and Clotho.
