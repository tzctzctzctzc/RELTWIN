\section{Conclusion}
RelTwin makes the correspondence between inverse queries and temporal windows explicit in audio grounding training. RBEE couples window preferences under relation exchange, while SetPO extends supervision to interval-set quality. Existing results place the largest SpotSound training-stage gain at RBEE and show improved controlled relation localization after SetPO. The full pipeline reaches 59.39\% SpotSound mIoU. These findings support relation-consistent training as a research direction while leaving exchange-specific attribution and natural-relation transfer unresolved.
