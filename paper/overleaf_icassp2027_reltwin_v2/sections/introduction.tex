\section{Introduction}
Audio temporal grounding retrieves the intervals in a recording that match a natural-language query. Text-to-audio grounding established temporal correspondence between phrases and events~\cite{xu2021grounding}, while audio moment retrieval evaluates language-based localization in untrimmed recordings~\cite{munakata2025amr}. SpotSound strengthens audio-language models through timestamp-interleaved representations and supervision for absent events~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. We study a complementary requirement: selecting the correct window when multiple windows contain the same sound categories but express different temporal relations.

Consider a recording containing both a dog bark followed by a bell and a bell followed by a dog bark. Recognizing the two categories cannot distinguish the answers. Reversing the query relation must change the selected window, although the waveform is unchanged. This motivates supervision of the \emph{correspondence between query transformations and localization decisions}, in addition to supervision of individual timestamp sequences. The example specifies a controlled task requirement; it does not assume that every existing model ignores temporal order.

We introduce RelTwin to instantiate this requirement with paired data and a coupled objective. Each composed recording contains both event orders, and each query has a matched inverse query. Relation--Boundary Exchange Equivariance (RBEE) combines sequence supervision, candidate discrimination, and a consistency penalty after exchanging candidate identities. Setwise Preference Optimization (SetPO) then replaces hard window labels with a quality distribution over structured interval sets, accounting for displaced boundaries and excessive coverage. Ordinary event-query rehearsal retains training signals for localization without relation words.

Equivariance and preference optimization are established ideas~\cite{cohen2016group,rafailov2023dpo}. Our focus is their task-specific formulation for inverse audio relations: a shared recording, an explicit candidate permutation, and interval-set quality supervision. RBEE is a soft training constraint, not an architecturally guaranteed equivariant model; SetPO uses listwise metric-derived targets rather than the DPO objective. We evaluate this formulation through existing matched training stages, class-disjoint relation diagnostics, and a separately identified full system. Verification and boundary refinement remain supporting inference steps, not the central contribution.
