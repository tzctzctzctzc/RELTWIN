\begin{abstract}
Audio temporal grounding must identify the event window specified by a query, not merely a window containing relevant sounds. We introduce RelTwin, a paired training framework that places opposite event orders in the same recording. Relation--Boundary Exchange Equivariance (RBEE) couples inverse queries by requiring their candidate-window preferences to exchange consistently. Setwise Preference Optimization (SetPO) extends this supervision to interval sets using temporal overlap and soft interval coverage. RBEE reaches 59.17\% mean mIoU across three seeds on SpotSound-Bench, 0.85 points above the reconstructed official baseline. On class-disjoint RelTwin development data, SetPO further improves mIoU by 3.36 points and paired localization accuracy by 5.21 points. The full SpotSound pipeline reaches 59.39\% mIoU, 1.49 points above the cited prior main-table best. We distinguish training-stage gains, controlled relation diagnostics, and system-level performance.
\end{abstract}
