\section{Conclusion}
We formulated local audio boundary refinement as a decision about utility relative to an existing answer, with an explicit identity action. Acoustic edit features, structured selection, and a bootstrap-ensemble criterion connect candidate boundaries to the choice of whether to revise an answer. The complete system exceeds the cited main-table mIoU results on SpotSound-Bench and Clotho-Moment. Metric-specific analysis and qualified transfer results delimit this evidence: local refinement is not a substitute for event recall or long-audio retrieval, and system-level gains do not establish component-level causality.
