\subsection{Candidate supervision improves binding}
RelTwin improves both relation selection and temporal overlap in the primary SFT comparison (Table~\ref{tab:training}). Relation mIoU increases from 74.58 to 88.55 ($+13.97$ points), with source-group 95\% CI $[9.84,17.51]$. JointPairAcc rises from 53.13 to 84.38 ($+31.25$ points; CI $[22.35,40.50]$), and Swap error falls from 40.00 to 7.50. On SpotSound, SFT scores 58.42 and RelTwin 59.43: $+1.02$ mIoU points with audio-group CI $[0.23,1.81]$. Shared positive examples and update counts connect these gains to adding explicit competition between timestamp answers. Across the 400 public queries, the paired win/tie/loss counts are 92/239/69, including two IoU decreases of at least 0.5.

Paired diagnosis reveals a change in answer selection. SFT returns identical answers for 37/160 inverse-query pairs (22 audios), compared with 1/160 for RelTwin; 27 of those 37 pairs become jointly correct. Overall, 51 pairs change from incorrect to correct, one in the opposite direction, and 24 remain incorrect. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this recovery: SFT returns the same interval for both queries, whereas RelTwin selects the corresponding relation window for each query. The measured set-IoU values retain the distinction between relation selection and boundary accuracy. JointPairAcc ties the changed answers to the correct relation targets.
