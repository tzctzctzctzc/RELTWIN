\subsection{Timestamp contrasts recover query-specific selection}
With matched data and updates, candidate supervision improves SpotSound mIoU by 1.02 points over paired SFT (95\% audio-group CI: $[0.23,1.81]$; Table~\ref{tab:training}).

JointPairAcc rises by 31.25 points (source-group CI $[22.35,40.50]$). Identical-answer pairs fall from 37 to 1 out of 160; 27 of those 37 become jointly correct. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this recovery. Across all pairs, transitions to joint correctness outnumber regressions by 51 to 1.

The relation mIoU gain is 13.97 points (CI $[9.84,17.51]$). Three-seed mean JointPairAcc is 81.46, with same-answer counts of 1, 0, and 0. Both windows contain the queried categories, so recovering distinct, jointly correct answers demonstrates relation selection beyond category presence.
