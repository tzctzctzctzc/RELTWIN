\subsection{Inverse-query selection across backbones}
On 300 inverse-query pairs (Table~\ref{tab:training}), JointPairAcc increases by 26.34 points with SpotSound-A candidate supervision and 8.00 points with full TimeAudio RelTwin. Both windows contain the queried categories, so joint correctness tests occurrence selection beyond category recognition. Fewer identical answers alone could reflect diversity; simultaneous reductions in swaps support correct query-specific selection.

\textit{Case analysis.} In the controlled example in Fig.~\ref{fig:pair}, paired SFT returns $[14.75,24.75]$\,s for both inverse queries. Candidate supervision moves the first prediction to $[1.6,8.7]$\,s, recovering the requested window (IoU $0\rightarrow0.69$), while retaining the correct inverse-query answer (IoU $0.98$). The corrected prediction still ends before the target at $11.75$\,s: selecting the right occurrence and recovering its full extent are distinct challenges.

\textit{Limitations.} Full TimeAudio RelTwin reduces same-answer pairs to 34/300, but swap error remains 54.33\%. Thus, different answers need not bind to the requested order. Answer competition improves selection without eliminating relation errors; broader temporal compositions and naturally occurring competing windows are important extensions.
