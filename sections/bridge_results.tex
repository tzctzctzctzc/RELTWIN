\subsection{Timestamp contrasts recover query-specific selection}
Paired SFT returns identical answers for 37/160 inverse-query pairs across 22 recordings; candidate supervision reduces this to one (Table~\ref{tab:training}). Of those 37 pairs, 27 become jointly correct: the two predictions recover their respective requested relations. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this query-specific recovery, with each inverse query selecting its corresponding event window.

JointPairAcc increases by 31.25 points (source-group CI $[22.35,40.50]$) and Swap error decreases by 32.50. Across all pairs, 51 become jointly correct and one regresses. Relation mIoU increases by 13.97 points (CI $[9.84,17.51]$), connecting selection with overlap.

Across three seeds, mean JointPairAcc is 81.46; same-answer counts are 1, 0, and 0.
