\subsection{Inverse-query selection across backbones}
On 300 inverse-query pairs (Table~\ref{tab:training}), JointPairAcc increases by 26.34 points with SpotSound-A candidate supervision and 8.00 points with full TimeAudio RelTwin. Both windows contain the queried categories, so joint correctness tests occurrence selection beyond category recognition. Fewer identical answers alone could reflect diversity; simultaneous reductions in swaps support correct query-specific selection.

\textit{Case analysis.} In the controlled example in Fig.~\ref{fig:pair}, paired SFT returns $[14.75,24.75]$\,s for both inverse queries. Candidate supervision moves the first prediction to $[1.6,8.7]$\,s, recovering the requested window (IoU $0\rightarrow0.69$), while retaining the correct inverse-query answer (IoU $0.98$). Correct occurrence selection does not automatically ensure complete temporal coverage: the corrected prediction ends before the target at $11.75$\,s. Finer-grained temporal supervision is a future direction for jointly improving occurrence selection and boundary localization.
