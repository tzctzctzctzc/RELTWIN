\subsection{Timestamp contrasts recover query-specific selection}
Paired SFT returns identical answers for 37/160 inverse-query pairs across 22 recordings; RelTwin reduces this to one (Table~\ref{tab:training}). Of those 37 pairs, 27 become jointly correct: differentiation recovers the requested relation, not just different timestamps. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this recovery; residual undercoverage distinguishes correct relation selection from exact boundaries.

JointPairAcc increases by 31.25 points (source-group CI $[22.35,40.50]$) and Swap error decreases by 32.50. Across all pairs, 51 become jointly correct, one regresses, and 24 remain incorrect. Relation mIoU increases by 13.97 points (CI $[9.84,17.51]$), connecting selection with overlap.

Across three RelTwin seeds, JointPairAcc averages 81.46 and identical-answer counts are 1, 0, and 0, reproducing the reduction in same-answer collapse.
