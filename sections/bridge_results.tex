\subsection{Inverse-query selection across backbones}
On 300 inverse-query pairs (Table~\ref{tab:training}), SpotSound-A's 256-update comparison isolates candidate supervision. Relation mIoU rises from 76.08 to 87.70 and JointPairAcc from 55.33 to 81.67; swap error falls from 36.33 to 7.00 and identical-answer pairs from 61 to 2.

TimeAudio compares paired SFT with complete two-stage RelTwin after 384+128 updates. Relation mIoU rises from 38.89 to 43.86 and JointPairAcc from 2.67 to 10.67; swap error falls from 78.33 to 54.33 and identical-answer pairs from 160 to 34.

Both windows contain the queried categories, so improved joint correctness supports occurrence selection beyond category recognition. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates SpotSound-A candidate supervision.

Fewer identical answers indicate query sensitivity; higher JointPairAcc confirms correct localization without window swaps. Together, they support query-specific selection beyond answer diversity.
