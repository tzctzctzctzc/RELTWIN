\subsection{Controlled ablation: complete-answer competition}
Under matched data and updates, paired SFT rewards both correct answers independently, whereas candidate supervision requires each to outrank the competing window. Candidate supervision improves SpotSound mIoU by 1.02 points over paired SFT (95\% audio-group CI: $[0.23,1.81]$; Table~\ref{tab:training}).

JointPairAcc rises by 31.25 points (source-group CI $[22.35,40.50]$). Identical-answer pairs fall from 37 to 1 out of 160; 27 of those 37 become jointly correct. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this recovery. Across all pairs, transitions to joint correctness outnumber regressions by 51 to 1.

The relation mIoU gain is 13.97 points (CI $[9.84,17.51]$). Because both candidate windows contain the queried categories, producing distinct, jointly correct answers demonstrates selection of the queried relation rather than category detection alone.
