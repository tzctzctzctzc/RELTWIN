\subsection{Timestamp contrasts recover query-specific selection}
Candidate supervision recovers relation-specific answers, raising JointPairAcc from 53.13 to 84.38 (Table~\ref{tab:training}). Identical-answer pairs fall from 37 to 1 out of 160; the original 37 span 22 recordings. Of these collapsed pairs, 27 become jointly correct, with both queries selecting their requested relations. Figure~\ref{fig:pair} illustrates this recovery.

The JointPairAcc gain is 31.25 points (source-group CI $[22.35,40.50]$), accompanied by a 32.50-point reduction in Swap error. Across all pairs, 51 become jointly correct and one regresses. Relation mIoU increases by 13.97 points (CI $[9.84,17.51]$), showing that improved relation selection also increases temporal overlap.

Across three seeds, mean JointPairAcc is 81.46; same-answer counts are 1, 0, and 0.
