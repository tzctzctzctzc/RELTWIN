\section{Conclusion}
RelTwin learns query--window binding through competition between acoustically valid timestamp answers. Gains across two backbones and three benchmarks, inverse-query selection, and silence interventions support this approach while preserving direct generation. The two training stages connect query-specific occurrence selection with interval-set quality: answer competition identifies the requested window, while graded preferences refine coverage, event count, and boundaries. Future work will extend answer competition to overlapping events and more complex temporal relations, and mine competing windows from natural recordings to broaden training coverage.
