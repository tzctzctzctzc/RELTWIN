\section{Conclusion}
RelTwin addresses query--window binding by contrasting acoustically valid timestamp answers with different relational meanings. It improves two backbones on three benchmarks, selects different correct windows for inverse queries, and retains the advantage after silence timing changes. The framework learns relation-aware occurrence selection while preserving direct timestamp generation.

Answer competition thus connects sound recognition to query-specific instance selection, distinguishing occurrences that share the same sound categories.
