\section{Conclusion}
Event presence alone does not determine which interval matches a relation query. RelTwin trains query--window binding through locally valid timestamp contrasts: competing windows have real acoustic support, but their correctness depends on the query. Paired diagnosis and the timing intervention show improved joint localization over paired SFT, alongside gains on SpotSound-Bench. These results support explicit query-conditioned competition between timestamp answers while retaining direct timestamp generation at inference.
