\subsection{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:protocol}
\textit{Datasets.}
We evaluate general localization on SpotSound-Bench (400 queries, 387 audios)~\cite{sun2026spotsound}, Clotho-Moment (6,649 queries), and the UnAV-100 subset (100 queries, 77 audios)~\cite{munakata2025amr}. For controlled relation learning, 40 ESC-50 classes produce 256 training recordings, 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs), and 512 category queries. The templates ``followed by'' and ``and then'' give two inverse-query pairs per recording. Table~\ref{tab:training} evaluates 300 inverse-query pairs (600 queries). The separate timing study uses the original development set: 10 held-out classes and 80 recordings, with classes and original Freesound IDs disjoint from adaptation data.

\textit{Evaluation.}
Public benchmarks use set-mIoU and R1 at IoU 0.3/0.5, with predictions clipped to the recording duration. For inverse-query pairs, let $u_{ij}=\setiou(\widehat W_i,W_j)$, where $\setiou(S,G)=|S\cap G|/|S\cup G|$ and an empty union scores zero. PairAcc requires $\min(u_{++},u_{--})\geq0.5$; a swap occurs if $u_{++}\leq u_{+-}$ or $u_{--}\leq u_{-+}$, including ties. JointPairAcc requires PairAcc without a swap. Inspired by paired compositional evaluation~\cite{thrush2022winoground}, it separates selection from coverage: the union of two disjoint targets can pass PairAcc but fails JointPairAcc. These are pair-level percentages; same-answer counts require identical interval lists.

\textit{Training and implementation.}
Both backbones train for 384 Stage~1 and 128 Stage~2 updates, with LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora}/bridge learning rates of $2\times10^{-6}/5\times10^{-6}$. Trainable parameters total 31.76M for TimeAudio and 37.63M for SpotSound-A; inference loads adapters and bridges. Candidate and exchange/JS weights are $\lambda=\gamma=1$, replay weight $\beta=0.75$, and reference-KL weight $\kappa=0.2$; $\tau=1$, $\tau_q=0.15$. Interval expansion is 15\% (minimum 0.05\,s). Decoding is greedy, with one beam and at most 128 new tokens.

In the TimeAudio training protocol, category queries target both occurrences of $A$ or $B$; category:relation pairs enter the paired objective at 3:1. Replay mixes Clotho-Moment training~\cite{munakata2025amr}, synthetic scale/cardinality data, TEMPO training~\cite{kulkarni2026tempo}, and AudioGrounding training~\cite{xu2021grounding} at 4:3:2:1.

The SpotSound-A comparison in Table~\ref{tab:training} isolates Stage~1 competition. Paired SFT uses $\lambda=0$ and candidate supervision $\lambda=1$, both with $\gamma=0$ and $\beta=0.5$. They share initialization, data, order, and 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates at $5\times10^{-6}$, with zero weight decay and gradient clipping~1. LoRA has rank~8, $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, and 20.19M trainable parameters. Each update accumulates one inverse-query pair and one replay query. Candidate supervision adds four gradient-free answer scores and weighted gradient recomputation to SFT's two positive-answer backpropagations. All variants use 16\,kHz mono audio, bfloat16, and direct timestamp generation.

For the timing intervention, we recompose 80 development recordings with a 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and a 2\,s tail while preserving waveforms, order, and queries. Frozen matched models evaluate one template (160 queries). Timing changes for 40 BA-first recordings; 40 AB-first recordings remain identical.

Both backbones' main results and matched point estimates use seed~0. Table~\ref{tab:training} reports single-checkpoint results; the TimeAudio comparison uses 384+128 updates for paired SFT and RelTwin. Timing-study CIs use 20,000 source-group bootstrap resamples from the original development grouping (39 connected groups; largest: 18 recordings).
