\section{Experimental Setup}
\textbf{Data and evaluation protocol.}
Adaptation uses 40 ESC-50 classes and 256 constructed recordings: 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs) and 512 rehearsal queries. The remaining 10 classes provide 80 recordings, 320 queries, and 160 inverse pairs for relation development. Training and development use disjoint classes and original Freesound IDs at the adaptation stage. The constructed split diagnoses query--window binding; public evaluation measures grounding performance on all 400 SpotSound queries over 387 audios. Both relation-development and public benchmark results informed method development.

\textbf{Training settings.}
Both methods retain SpotSound-A's LoRA adaptation~\cite{hu2022lora} on Audio Flamingo~3, with rank~8, scaling parameter $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, and approximately 20.19M trainable adapter parameters. SFT and RelTwin use 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates, zero weight decay, learning rate $5\times10^{-6}$, gradient clipping 1.0, $\tau=1$, and $\beta=0.5$; RelTwin uses $\lambda=1$. Each update processes one inverse-query pair (two relation queries) and one category-rehearsal query. Gradients accumulate over these components, with no accumulation across pairs. The paired comparison uses seed~0, shared initialization, example order, training code, and software environment to assess the effect of candidate supervision. Updates are matched, but FLOPs and wall time are not: RelTwin first scores four query--answer combinations without gradients and then recomputes weighted gradients, whereas SFT backpropagates through the two positive answers. Both include rehearsal.

\textbf{Inference.}
We evaluate paired SFT seed~0 and RelTwin seeds 0/1/2 after 256 updates. SpotSound-A and the adapted models share the inference protocol: 16\,kHz mono audio, bfloat16 inference, and greedy decoding (one beam, no sampling), with at most 128 new tokens per query.

\textbf{Comparison roles.}
Table~\ref{tab:main} compares RelTwin with published SpotSound-Bench results; Table~\ref{tab:training} separates the backbone reference, matched training comparison, and three-seed replication. The backbone scores 58.42 mIoU under our inference protocol, distinct from its published 57.9. The $+1.26$ improvement in Table~\ref{tab:main} is measured against the published main-table best.

\textbf{Statistical analysis.}
We compute 95\% confidence intervals from 20,000 bootstrap resamples, keeping queries from the same audio together. Relation comparisons additionally resample 39 connected groups of recordings sharing original sources (largest: 18 audios), keeping reused excerpts within each group. These intervals quantify sample uncertainty for the evaluated seeds, without correction for development selection.
