\subsection{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:protocol}
\textit{Datasets.}
We evaluate general localization on SpotSound-Bench (400 queries, 387 audios)~\cite{sun2026spotsound}, Clotho-Moment (6,649 queries), and the UnAV-100 subset (100 queries, 77 audios)~\cite{munakata2025amr}. For controlled relation learning, 40 ESC-50 classes produce 256 training recordings, 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs), and 512 category queries. The templates ``followed by'' and ``and then'' give two inverse-query pairs per recording. Table~\ref{tab:training} evaluates 300 inverse-query pairs (600 queries). The separate timing study uses the original development set: 10 held-out classes and 80 recordings, with classes and original Freesound IDs disjoint from adaptation data.

\textit{Evaluation.}
Public benchmarks use set-mIoU and R1 at IoU 0.3/0.5, with predictions clipped to the recording duration. For inverse-query pairs, let $u_{ij}=\setiou(\widehat W_i,W_j)$, where $\setiou(S,G)=|S\cap G|/|S\cup G|$ and an empty union scores zero. PairAcc requires $\min(u_{++},u_{--})\geq0.5$; a swap occurs if $u_{++}\leq u_{+-}$ or $u_{--}\leq u_{-+}$, including ties. JointPairAcc requires PairAcc without a swap. Inspired by paired compositional evaluation~\cite{thrush2022winoground}, it separates selection from coverage: the union of two disjoint targets can pass PairAcc but fails JointPairAcc. These are pair-level percentages; same-answer counts require identical interval lists.

\textit{Training and implementation.}
Table~\ref{tab:main} uses full RelTwin on both backbones: 384+128 Stage~1/2 updates; LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} and bridge rates $2\times10^{-6}/5\times10^{-6}$; 31.76M/37.63M trainable parameters for TimeAudio/SpotSound-A. Inference loads adapters and bridges. Candidate/exchange weights are $\lambda=\gamma=1$, replay $\beta=0.75$, reference-KL $\kappa=0.2$, and temperatures $\tau=1$, $\tau_q=0.15$. Interval expansion is 15\% (minimum 0.05\,s). Greedy decoding uses one beam and at most 128 new tokens.

In the TimeAudio training protocol, category queries target both occurrences of $A$ or $B$; category:relation pairs enter the paired objective at 3:1. Replay mixes Clotho-Moment training~\cite{munakata2025amr}, synthetic scale/cardinality data, TEMPO training~\cite{kulkarni2026tempo}, and AudioGrounding training~\cite{xu2021grounding} at 4:3:2:1.

Table~\ref{tab:training}'s SpotSound-A ablation isolates Stage~1 competition. Paired SFT uses $\lambda=0$ and candidate supervision $\lambda=1$, both with $\gamma=0$ and $\beta=0.5$. Both share initialization, data/order, and 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates at $5\times10^{-6}$, with zero weight decay and gradient clipping~1. LoRA: rank~8, $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, 20.19M trainable parameters. Updates accumulate one inverse-query pair and one replay query. All variants use 16\,kHz mono audio, bfloat16, and direct timestamp generation.

Timing intervention preserves waveforms, order, and queries in 80 development recordings, using a 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and a 2\,s tail. Frozen matched models evaluate one template (160 queries): 40 BA-first recordings change; 40 AB-first recordings remain identical.

Table~\ref{tab:training}'s TimeAudio runs share official initialization, data, order, and 384+128 updates: full RelTwin versus SFT with $\lambda=0$ (competition, JS, and set preferences off; Eqs.~\ref{eq:loss}, \ref{eq:stage2}). Main and matched results use single checkpoints with seed~0. Timing CIs use 20,000 source-group bootstrap resamples of the original development groups (39; largest: 18 recordings).
