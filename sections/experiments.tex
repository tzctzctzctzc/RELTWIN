\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:protocol}
\textbf{Public benchmarks.}
We evaluate general localization on SpotSound-Bench (400 queries, 387 audios)~\cite{sun2026spotsound}, Clotho-Moment (6,649 queries), and UnAV-100 (100 queries, 77 audios)~\cite{munakata2025amr}. We report set-mIoU and R1 at IoU 0.3/0.5, clipping intervals to recording duration.

\textbf{Twin data and relation evaluation.}
Forty ESC-50 classes yield 256 training recordings, 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs), and 512 category queries. Templates ``followed by'' and ``and then'' give two inverse-query pairs per recording. Relation development uses 10 held-out classes, 80 recordings, and 320 queries (160 pairs), with disjoint classes and original Freesound IDs at adaptation. It tests query-specific selection; timing intervention tests persistence under changed silence (Section~\ref{sec:timing}).

\textbf{Joint localization and relation selection.}
For interval unions, $\setiou(S,G)=|S\cap G|/|S\cup G|$ (zero for an empty union). Given predictions $\widehat W_i$, let $u_{ij}=\setiou(\widehat W_i,W_j)$. PairAcc requires $\min(u_{++},u_{--})\geq0.5$. Swap error occurs if $u_{++}\leq u_{+-}$ or $u_{--}\leq u_{-+}$, including ties. JointPairAcc requires PairAcc success without Swap error. Inspired by paired compositional evaluation~\cite{thrush2022winoground}, it distinguishes query-specific selection from coverage: returning the union of two disjoint, equal-length targets passes PairAcc (IoU $0.5$) but fails JointPairAcc. We report pair-level percentages; same-answer counts require identical parsed interval lists.

\textbf{RelTwin training.}
TimeAudio starts from its official checkpoint with 384/128 updates in Stages~1/2. Its 31.76M trainable parameters use learning rates $2\times10^{-6}$ for LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} and $5\times10^{-6}$ for temporal/audio connectors. Category queries target both occurrences of $A$ or $B$; category- and relation-query pairs enter the paired objective at 3:1. Replay mixes Clotho-Moment training~\cite{munakata2025amr}, synthetic scale/cardinality, TEMPO training~\cite{kulkarni2026tempo}, and AudioGrounding training~\cite{xu2021grounding} at 4:3:2:1. We use $\lambda=\gamma=1$, $\beta=0.75$, $\kappa=0.2$, $\tau=1$, $\tau_q=0.15$, and 15\% interval expansion (minimum 0.05\,s).

\textbf{Matched mechanism experiment.}
Table~\ref{tab:training} isolates $\mathcal L_{\rm cand}$ on SpotSound-A after Stage~1: $\gamma=0$, $\beta=0.5$, and $\lambda=0$ (paired SFT) or 1 (candidate supervision). Both share initialization, relation/rehearsal data, data order, and 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates at $5\times10^{-6}$, with zero weight decay and gradient clipping~1. LoRA uses rank~8, $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, and 20.19M parameters. Each update accumulates one pair and one rehearsal query. Candidate supervision adds four gradient-free scores and weighted gradient recomputation to SFT's two positive-answer backpropagations.

\textbf{Inference.}
Decoding is greedy, one beam, with at most 128 new tokens. The mechanism experiment uses 16\,kHz mono audio and bfloat16.

\textbf{Comparison and statistics.}
Table~\ref{tab:main} uses published baseline scores; Table~\ref{tab:training} evaluates the backbone and training variants under shared inference. Relation-development and public benchmark scores informed development. TimeAudio uses seed~0. Mechanism point estimates use seed~0, selected by SpotSound mIoU among five runs, with matched SFT; Table~\ref{tab:training}(c) separately reports seeds 0/1/2 as mean $\pm$ sample SD.

We use 20,000 audio-group bootstrap resamples for 95\% CIs; relation tests resample 39 connected source-sharing groups (largest: 18 audios). The intervals quantify sampling variation for the selected checkpoints.
