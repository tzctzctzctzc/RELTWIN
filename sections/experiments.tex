\subsection{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:protocol}
\textit{Datasets.}
We evaluate general localization on SpotSound-Bench (400 queries, 387 audios)~\cite{sun2026spotsound}, Clotho-Moment (6,649 queries), and the UnAV-100 subset (100 queries, 77 audios)~\cite{munakata2025amr}. For controlled relation learning, 40 ESC-50 classes produce 256 training recordings, 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs), and 512 category queries. The templates ``followed by'' and ``and then'' give two inverse-query pairs per recording. Relation development uses 10 held-out classes, 80 recordings, and 320 queries (160 pairs), with classes and original Freesound IDs disjoint from adaptation data.

\textit{Evaluation.}
Public benchmarks use set-mIoU and R1 at IoU 0.3/0.5, with predictions clipped to the recording duration. For inverse-query pairs, let $u_{ij}=\setiou(\widehat W_i,W_j)$, where $\setiou(S,G)=|S\cap G|/|S\cup G|$ and an empty union scores zero. PairAcc requires $\min(u_{++},u_{--})\geq0.5$; a swap occurs if $u_{++}\leq u_{+-}$ or $u_{--}\leq u_{-+}$, including ties. JointPairAcc requires PairAcc without a swap. Inspired by paired compositional evaluation~\cite{thrush2022winoground}, it separates selection from coverage: the union of two disjoint targets can pass PairAcc but fails JointPairAcc. These are pair-level percentages; same-answer counts require identical interval lists.

\textit{Training and implementation.}
The complete RelTwin configuration uses 384 Stage~1 and 128 Stage~2 updates for both backbones. LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} and bridge learning rates are $2\times10^{-6}$ and $5\times10^{-6}$; exchange/JS weight is~1, replay weight~0.75, and reference-KL weight~0.2. TimeAudio and SpotSound-A use 31.76M and 37.63M trainable parameters, respectively; both use seed~0 and load adapter+bridge weights at inference. We use $\lambda=\gamma=1$, $\beta=0.75$, $\kappa=0.2$, $\tau=1$, $\tau_q=0.15$, and 15\% interval expansion with a 0.05\,s minimum. Decoding is greedy with one beam and at most 128 new tokens.

Table~\ref{tab:training} isolates Stage~1 competition on SpotSound-A. Paired SFT uses $\lambda=0$ and candidate supervision $\lambda=1$, both with $\gamma=0$ and $\beta=0.5$. They share initialization, data, order, and 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates at $5\times10^{-6}$, with zero weight decay and gradient clipping~1. LoRA has rank~8, $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, and 20.19M trainable parameters. Each update accumulates one inverse-query pair and one replay query. Candidate supervision adds four gradient-free answer scores and weighted gradient recomputation to SFT's two positive-answer backpropagations. All variants use 16\,kHz mono audio, bfloat16, and direct timestamp generation.

For the timing intervention, we recompose 80 development recordings with a 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and a 2\,s tail while preserving waveforms, order, and queries. Frozen matched models evaluate one template (160 queries). Timing changes for 40 BA-first recordings; 40 AB-first recordings remain identical.

TimeAudio and matched point estimates use seed~0; Table~\ref{tab:training}(c) reports candidate supervision over seeds 0/1/2 as mean $\pm$ sample SD. We obtain 95\% CIs from 20,000 audio-group bootstrap resamples; relation tests resample 39 connected source-sharing groups (largest: 18 recordings).
