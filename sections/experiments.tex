\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:protocol}
\textbf{Data and evaluation protocol.}
Core SpotSound-A adaptation uses 40 ESC-50 classes and 256 recordings: 1,024 relation queries (512 pairs) and 512 rehearsal queries. Ten held-out classes provide 80 recordings, 320 queries, and 160 inverse pairs for relation development, with disjoint classes and original Freesound IDs at adaptation. Evaluation covers all 400 SpotSound-Bench queries over 387 audios, 6,649 Clotho-Moment queries, and UnAV-100's 100 queries over 77 audios~\cite{munakata2025amr}. We report set-mIoU and R1 at IoU 0.3/0.5, clipping intervals to duration. Relation-development and public scores informed method development.

\textbf{SpotSound-A training.}
SFT and RelTwin initialize from SpotSound-A on Audio Flamingo~3. LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} uses rank~8, $\alpha=16$, dropout~0.1, and 20.19M parameters. Both run 256 AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} updates at $5\times10^{-6}$, zero weight decay, gradient clipping~1, $\tau=1$, $\beta=0.5$; RelTwin uses $\lambda=1$. Each update accumulates one inverse-query pair and one rehearsal query. Initialization, data order, code, environment, and updates are matched. RelTwin computes four gradient-free scores then weighted gradient recomputation; SFT backpropagates through two positive answers. Both include rehearsal.

\textbf{TimeAudio full configuration.}
Official TimeAudio, seed~0, receives 384 candidate-training updates with Jensen--Shannon consistency under inverse-query label exchange, then 128 SetPO updates (interval-set listwise preferences with quality-based soft targets). Its 31.76M parameters use rates $2\times10^{-6}$ (LoRA) and $5\times10^{-6}$ (temporal/audio connectors). Event/relation twins mix 3:1; replay mixes Clotho training, synthetic scale/cardinality, TEMPO training, and AudioGrounding training at 4:3:2:1.

\textbf{Inference.}
Both backbones use greedy one-beam decoding, capped at 128 new tokens. SpotSound-A uses 16\,kHz mono audio and bfloat16; post-development timing tests use frozen matched checkpoints (Section~\ref{sec:timing}).
