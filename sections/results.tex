\subsection{Grounding performance across benchmarks}
On SpotSound-Bench, Clotho-Moment, and UnAV-100, SpotSound-A + RelTwin reaches 59.43, 86.68, and 74.03 mIoU (Table~\ref{tab:main}), exceeding its published baseline by 1.53, 1.08, and 4.23 points. Full-configuration TimeAudio reaches 18.36, 34.08, and 30.48, with gains of 8.16, 5.48, and 14.48 points. Each backbone uses one adapted checkpoint across benchmarks.

On the same 400 SpotSound queries, RelTwin gains 1.02 mIoU over matched paired SFT (audio-group CI $[0.23,1.81]$): 92 wins, 239 ties, and 69 losses. Its three-seed mean is 59.16 (Table~\ref{tab:training}c). We next examine responses to inverse queries.

\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
To test layout-specific silence, we recompose 80 development audios with 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and 2\,s tail, preserving waveforms, order, and queries. The frozen matched models evaluate one template (160 queries). Timing changes for 40 BA-first recordings; 40 AB-first recordings remain waveform-identical.

On changed recordings, RelTwin reaches 87.50 JointPairAcc against SFT's 67.50 ($+20.00$; CI $[2.94,33.33]$); originally they scored 85.00 and 42.50. RelTwin retains a 20-point selection advantage under the altered timing, supporting query--window binding across both silence layouts. Across all 160 queries, SFT and RelTwin score 81.34 and 90.65 mIoU.

\subsection{Why locally valid answers matter}
Shared excerpts hold event identity fixed while inverse queries exchange the correct window. Detecting both categories supports both candidates; selecting the requested order requires query-conditioned comparison. RelTwin's reduced same-answer collapse and improved JointPairAcc capture this distinction in the controlled binding test. Timestamp-answer supervision preserves direct generation at inference.
