\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
On the 40 recordings with altered silence timing, candidate supervision retains a 20-point JointPairAcc advantage (87.50 vs.\ 67.50; CI $[2.94,33.33]$), compared with original scores of 85.00 and 42.50. The advantage persists when event waveforms, order, and queries are fixed but silence timing changes.

Together, public gains test transfer, paired tests isolate selection, and timing changes test position-independent relation learning; set-level learning extends this decision to coverage and boundaries.
