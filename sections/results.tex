\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
The timing intervention tests whether layout-specific silence explains the binding advantage. We recompose all 80 development audios with 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gap, 3\,s between windows, and 2\,s tail, preserving event waveforms, order, and queries. Frozen SFT/RelTwin seed~0 models evaluate one template (160 queries). The 40 BA-first recordings receive the changed timing layout; the other 40 AB-first recordings remain waveform-identical.

On the 40 changed recordings, RelTwin achieves 87.50 JointPairAcc versus 67.50 for SFT: $+20.00$ points, source-group CI $[2.94,33.33]$. Under the original layout, the corresponding scores are 85.00 and 42.50. The intervention improves SFT and narrows the gap from 42.50 to 20.00 points, showing that timing layout affects the comparison. RelTwin nevertheless retains an advantage when the silence pattern changes. Across all 160 queries, uniform-timing mIoU is 81.34 for SFT and 90.65 for RelTwin. Together, these post-development results show that the original silence pattern does not fully explain the query--window binding gain.

\subsection{Public grounding performance}
RelTwin attains the highest scores on all three metrics among the methods in Table~\ref{tab:main}. Across three training seeds, mIoU averages 59.16, exceeding the prior main-table best of 57.9 by 1.26 points. R1@0.3 and R1@0.5 improve by 0.67 and 2.58 points, respectively. The larger recall gain at IoU~0.5 shows that the improvement extends beyond loose overlap to predictions satisfying the stricter localization criterion. The strongest published comparator, SpotSound-A, uses the same Audio Flamingo~3 backbone, placing these gains against a model already adapted for temporal grounding.

The matched training comparison in Table~\ref{tab:training} complements these published-score comparisons. On the same 400 public queries, RelTwin improves over paired SFT by 1.02 mIoU points, with audio-group CI $[0.23,1.81]$. The paired outcomes are 92 improvements, 239 ties, and 69 regressions, including two IoU decreases of at least 0.5. Public localization gains therefore accompany the recovery of query-specific selection observed in the controlled relation diagnostic.

\subsection{Scope}
Repeated excerpts and fixed templates enable controlled binding tests. Alternative hard-negative constructions, matched multi-seed replication, and natural overlapping or long-range relations remain open evaluations. Adaptation-stage source separation does not audit backbone pretraining overlap.
