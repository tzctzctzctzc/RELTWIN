\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
To test layout-specific silence, we recompose 80 development audios with 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and 2\,s tail, preserving waveforms, order, and queries. The frozen matched models evaluate one template (160 queries). Timing changes for 40 BA-first recordings; 40 AB-first recordings remain waveform-identical.

On changed recordings, RelTwin reaches 87.50 JointPairAcc against SFT's 67.50 ($+20.00$; CI $[2.94,33.33]$); originally they scored 85.00 and 42.50. RelTwin retains a 20-point selection advantage under the altered timing, supporting query--occurrence binding across both silence layouts. Across all 160 queries, SFT and RelTwin score 81.34 and 90.65 mIoU.

\subsection{Why locally valid answers matter}
Shared excerpts hold event identity fixed while inverse queries exchange the correct window. Detecting both categories supports both candidates; selecting the requested order requires query-conditioned comparison. RelTwin's reduced same-answer collapse and improved JointPairAcc capture this distinction in the controlled binding test. Timestamp-answer supervision preserves direct generation at inference.
