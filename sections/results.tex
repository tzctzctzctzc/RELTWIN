\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
On the 40 recordings with altered silence timing, candidate supervision retains a 20-point JointPairAcc advantage (87.50 vs.\ 67.50; CI $[2.94,33.33]$), compared with original scores of 85.00 and 42.50. The advantage persists when event waveforms, order, and queries are fixed but silence timing changes.

Fixed silence duration or window placement therefore cannot solely explain the binding gain. Public benchmarks test general localization. The matched ablation isolates complete-answer competition, while temporal recomposition tests whether relation selection survives changed timing. Stage~2 extends this competition from occurrence choice to interval coverage, count, and boundaries.
