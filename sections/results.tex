\subsection{Binding gains persist after timing intervention}
\label{sec:timing}
To test layout-specific silence, we recompose 80 development audios with 1.5\,s lead-in, 0.25\,s within-window gaps, 3\,s between windows, and 2\,s tail, preserving waveforms, order, and queries. Frozen seed-0 models evaluate one template (160 queries). Timing changes for 40 BA-first recordings; 40 AB-first recordings remain waveform-identical.

On changed recordings, RelTwin reaches 87.50 JointPairAcc against SFT's 67.50 ($+20.00$; CI $[2.94,33.33]$); originally they scored 85.00 and 42.50. The gap narrows but remains positive, supporting binding beyond the original silence layout. Across all 160 queries, SFT and RelTwin score 81.34 and 90.65 mIoU. This diagnostic follows method development.

\subsection{System performance and training transfer}
NOVA reaches 59.39 SpotSound mIoU (Table~\ref{tab:main}), exceeding the prior main-table best by 1.49 points; R1@.3/.5 improve by 2.75/3.00. Clotho reaches 86.86 mIoU ($+1.26$), while recalls trail prior bests. UnAV reaches 73.46 ($+1.06$), subject to the release qualification in Section~\ref{sec:protocol}.

RelTwin's matched comparison supplies training attribution: $+1.02$ SpotSound mIoU over SFT (CI $[0.23,1.81]$), with 92 wins, 239 ties, and 69 losses, including two drops $\geq0.5$ IoU. Three-seed mIoU is 59.16, exceeding published 57.9 by 1.26. Clotho/UnAV instead start at 86.8543/73.4513 with official predictions, gaining 0.0013/0.0133 from refinement. These system totals and controlled training gains answer different questions: deployment performance and candidate supervision's effect.

\subsection{Scope}
Repeated excerpts and fixed templates enable controlled binding tests. Alternative hard-negative constructions, matched multi-seed replication, and natural overlapping or long-range relations remain open evaluations. Adaptation-stage source separation does not audit backbone pretraining overlap.
