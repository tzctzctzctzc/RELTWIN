\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Audio temporal grounding.}
Weakly supervised grounding learns temporal correspondences from clip-level captions~\cite{xu2024wstag}. Audio-language models such as Pengi~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi}, Qwen2-Audio~\cite{chu2024qwen2audio}, and Audio Flamingo~3~\cite{goel2025af3} provide audio-conditioned text generation. TimeAudio introduces temporal markers and absolute time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}, while SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and uses absent-event queries to suppress hallucinated intervals~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. RelTwin adapts grounding models to select among acoustically supported windows according to the queried relation.

\textbf{Compositional audio--language learning.}
CLAP aligns audio and text in a shared representation space~\cite{wu2023clap}. T-CLAP introduces order-reversed descriptions and a temporally focused contrastive objective~\cite{yuan2024tclap}. CompA evaluates event order and attribute binding, and trains CompA-CLAP with composition-aware hard negatives~\cite{ghosh2024compa}. CoSTALA combines spatio-temporal contrast with local event--caption alignment and feature consistency~\cite{ren2026costala}. These methods learn compositional audio--text representations. RelTwin supervises relative scores of complete timestamp answers.

\textbf{Hard negatives for temporal grounding.}
In compositional video grounding, SHINE combines generated hard-negative queries with within-video and cross-query saliency ranking~\cite{cheng2024shine}. RelTwin's competing answer has real acoustic support but mismatches the requested relation. It becomes the correct answer when the query reverses, providing a paired contrast within a fixed audio context.
