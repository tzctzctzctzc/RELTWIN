Prior work advances several parts of audio grounding. Weakly supervised methods learn localization from clip-level captions~\cite{xu2024wstag}, while audio-language models support open-ended generation~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi,chu2024qwen2audio,goel2025af3}. TimeAudio adds temporal markers and absolute time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}; SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and trains on absent-event queries~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. These systems improve timestamp generation, but their positive sequence objectives do not explicitly compare two acoustically valid answers.

Compositional audio--language methods distinguish alternative descriptions through audio--text alignment~\cite{wu2023clap}, counterfactual captions~\cite{vosoughi2024counterfactual}, temporal contrast~\cite{yuan2024tclap}, composition-aware negatives~\cite{ghosh2024compa}, and multi-grain alignment~\cite{ren2026costala}. SHINE constructs negative queries and saliency rankings for video grounding~\cite{cheng2024shine}. RelTwin instead scores complete timestamp answers for coexisting windows whose correctness exchanges with the query.
