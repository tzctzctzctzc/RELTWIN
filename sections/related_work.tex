\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Audio temporal grounding.}
Weakly supervised grounding learns from clip-level captions~\cite{xu2024wstag}, while audio-language models enable text generation~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi,chu2024qwen2audio,goel2025af3}. TimeAudio adds temporal markers and absolute time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}; SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and trains on absent-event queries~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. RelTwin addresses query-dependent selection among \emph{present} windows.

\textbf{Compositional audio--language learning.}
CLAP aligns audio and text in a shared representation space~\cite{wu2023clap}. Counterfactual caption learning separates alternative descriptions while preserving factual audio--text consistency~\cite{vosoughi2024counterfactual}. T-CLAP contrasts temporal order~\cite{yuan2024tclap}; CompA evaluates order and attribute binding and trains with composition-aware negatives~\cite{ghosh2024compa}. CoSTALA combines spatio-temporal contrast, local alignment, and feature consistency~\cite{ren2026costala}. RelTwin instead supervises complete timestamp answers: both candidates are grounded in the recording, and their target roles exchange with the query.

\textbf{Hard negatives for temporal grounding.}
SHINE uses generated negative queries and saliency ranking for compositional video grounding~\cite{cheng2024shine}. RelTwin contrasts complete answer likelihoods in a fixed audio context, retaining the grounder's direct generation.
