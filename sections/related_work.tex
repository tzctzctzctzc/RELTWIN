Weakly supervised grounding learns from clip-level captions~\cite{xu2024wstag}; audio-language models support open-ended generation~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi,chu2024qwen2audio,goel2025af3}. TimeAudio introduces temporal markers and time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}; SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and trains on absent-event queries~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. AudioTime evaluates temporal control in audio generation~\cite{xie2024audiotime}, whereas TAG-Bench evaluates query-based localization, exposing missed occurrences and imprecise boundaries~\cite{dai2026tagbench}.

Compositional methods distinguish descriptions through audio--text alignment~\cite{wu2023clap}, counterfactual captions~\cite{vosoughi2024counterfactual}, temporal contrast~\cite{yuan2024tclap}, composition-aware negatives~\cite{ghosh2024compa}, and multi-grain alignment~\cite{ren2026costala}. SHINE constructs negative queries and saliency rankings for video grounding~\cite{cheng2024shine}. AHA motivates negative constraints beyond SFT, using direct preference optimization~\cite{rafailov2023dpo} to contrast factual answers with hallucinations, including temporal relation errors~\cite{chen2026aha}. RelTwin instead contrasts complete timestamp answers for coexisting acoustic windows, exchanging their positive and negative roles when the query reverses.
