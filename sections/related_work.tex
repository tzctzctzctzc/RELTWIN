\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Audio temporal grounding.}
Weakly supervised grounding learns temporal correspondences from clip-level captions, reducing dependence on frame-level annotations~\cite{xu2024wstag}. Audio-language models such as Pengi~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi}, Qwen2-Audio~\cite{chu2024qwen2audio}, and Audio Flamingo~3~\cite{goel2025af3} provide audio-conditioned text generation. For temporal localization, TimeAudio introduces temporal markers and absolute time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}, while SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and uses absent-event queries to suppress hallucinated intervals~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. SpotSound thus combines event-presence supervision with timestamp prediction. RelTwin targets query-specific selection when inverse relations coexist: both descriptions have acoustic support in the recording, and the requested relation determines which occurrence is the answer.

\textbf{Compositional audio--language learning.}
CLAP aligns audio and text in a shared representation space~\cite{wu2023clap}. T-CLAP introduces order-reversed descriptions and a temporal-focused contrastive objective~\cite{yuan2024tclap}. CompA evaluates event order and attribute binding, and trains CompA-CLAP with composition-aware hard negatives and modular contrastive learning~\cite{ghosh2024compa}. CoSTALA combines spatio-temporal contrast with local event--caption alignment and feature consistency~\cite{ren2026costala}. These methods organize audio--text representations around compositional relations, including their local structure. RelTwin places the supervision on complete timestamp answer sequences: their query-conditioned likelihoods determine the preference between competing windows. This trains relational selection in the output space of a generative grounding model.

\textbf{Hard negatives for temporal grounding.}
In compositional video grounding, SHINE combines generated hard-negative queries with within-video and cross-query saliency ranking~\cite{cheng2024shine}. Its training compares segment relevance as well as query compositions. RelTwin makes complete timestamp answers the candidates for comparison within one recording. Each candidate denotes an entire event sequence with real acoustic support. Under a fixed query, one answer matches the requested order and the other answers the inverse relation; reversing the query exchanges their labels while preserving the audio context. Comparing the length-normalized sequence log-likelihoods explicitly binds each relation query to its corresponding local answer.
