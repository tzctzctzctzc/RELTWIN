Existing methods improve several parts of grounding. Weakly supervised models learn from clip-level captions~\cite{xu2024wstag}, while audio-language models support audio-conditioned text generation~\cite{deshmukh2023pengi,chu2024qwen2audio,goel2025af3}. TimeAudio introduces temporal markers and absolute time-aware encoding~\cite{wang2026timeaudio}; SpotSound interleaves timestamps with audio tokens and trains on queries for absent events~\cite{sun2026spotsound}. CLAP aligns audio and text~\cite{wu2023clap}; counterfactual caption learning~\cite{vosoughi2024counterfactual}, T-CLAP~\cite{yuan2024tclap}, and CompA~\cite{ghosh2024compa} address distinctions between descriptions, including event order and composition. CoSTALA combines spatio-temporal contrast with local alignment and feature consistency~\cite{ren2026costala}. For video grounding, SHINE uses negative queries and saliency ranking~\cite{cheng2024shine}. RelTwin focuses on a different supervision target: the complete timestamp answers for competing windows in the same recording.
