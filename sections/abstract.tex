\begin{abstract}
Audio temporal grounding must identify which occurrence answers a query when several acoustically valid windows coexist. Event presence alone does not determine which occurrence matches the requested relation. We introduce NOVA, combining relation-aware candidate generation, audio verification, and selective boundary refinement. Its core training module, RelTwin, learns query--occurrence binding through locally valid timestamp contrasts: shared excerpts form opposite-order windows whose correct and incorrect roles reverse with the query. Competing timestamp answers therefore differ in the requested relation, not acoustic validity. RelTwin reaches 59.43 mIoU on SpotSound-Bench and improves over matched SFT by 1.02 points, while joint localization improves by 31.25 points on synthetic relation-development data. NOVA reaches 86.86 mIoU on Clotho-Moment and 73.46 on the released UnAV subset. The binding advantage persists under timing intervention, supporting explicit supervision of which occurrence answers the query.
\end{abstract}
