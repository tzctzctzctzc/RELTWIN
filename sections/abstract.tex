\begin{abstract}
Audio temporal grounding must select the occurrence described by a query, even when opposite event orders coexist in one recording. NOVA combines relation-aware candidate generation, audio verification, and selective boundary refinement. We focus on RelTwin's locally valid timestamp supervision: shared excerpts form opposite-order windows whose answers compete under each query. Both windows have acoustic support; the requested relation determines the correct answer. NOVA reaches 59.39 and 86.86 mIoU on SpotSound-Bench and Clotho-Moment, exceeding the respective prior main-table bests by 1.49 and 1.26 points, and scores 73.46 on the released UnAV subset. In matched SFT comparisons, RelTwin improves SpotSound mIoU by 1.02 points and joint localization by 31.25 points on synthetic relation-development data. The binding advantage persists under timing intervention, supporting explicit query-conditioned competition between timestamp answers.
\end{abstract}
